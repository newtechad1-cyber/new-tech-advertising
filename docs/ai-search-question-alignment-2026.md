# NTA Knowledge Library: 2026 question alignment

Research and content work as of October 7, 2026. Audience: U.S. small-business owners, especially local service businesses. This is an editorial priority set, not a verified ranking of the most searched questions.

## What the evidence can establish

Public research reviewed here measures business adoption, use cases, concerns, and intended AI discovery activity. It does not provide comparable 2026 U.S. monthly search volumes for these exact questions. Wording in the table is drawn from NTA's existing questions; priority is an editorial inference from the research and relevance to NTA. Do not label the table “Google's top questions” or attach invented volumes. Research through October 7 is not a full-year 2026 ranking.

### Primary research

- [Bluevine 2026 Small Business AI Trends Report](https://www.bluevine.com/blog/small-business-ai-trends-report-2026), published July 15. Centiment surveyed 942 U.S. owners or majority owners of businesses with 2–249 employees and revenue of $50,000–$5 million, April 7–9. The sample is unweighted. Among reported use cases, data analysis/business insights (39%), marketing/sales (37%), operations (29%), and customer service (27%) stood out. Privacy/security (33%) and accuracy distrust (31%) were leading barriers. These are survey findings, not query volumes or guaranteed outcomes. Separate Bluevine customer transaction data is not representative of all small businesses.
- [U.S. Chamber 2026 Empowering Small Business report](https://www.uschamber.com/technology/2026-empowering-small-business-report), published October 2. Teneo surveyed 3,732 U.S. businesses with fewer than 250 employees, July 23–August 6. The report describes marketing, customer experience, customer relationships, insights, and communication among AI uses. It supports those topic clusters, not the precise ranking or wording of questions.
- [Intuit QuickBooks 2026 Holiday Shopping Survey](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/small-business-data/holiday-shopping-survey/). The small-business sample was 1,002 U.S. owners, managers, and decision-makers at businesses with 0–99 employees, surveyed through Dynata in August. It reports 46% had taken steps to be easier to find through AI search or shopping and another 33% planned to. This is a seasonal survey about reported actions and intentions, not proof of visibility or a local-service search-volume estimate.
- [Google's generative AI search optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide?version=published). Google favors useful, distinctive content with experience and clear organization; standard SEO remains relevant. It cautions against pages created for every query variation. Google visibility requires indexing and snippet eligibility, and is not guaranteed. This source guides implementation rather than measuring demand.
