# Startup and Entrepreneur Search Language

## Purpose

Extend NTA's practical business education to aspiring entrepreneurs without weakening its established position with existing small-business owners.

This is an audience path, not a keyword-stuffing project. The language should appear where it genuinely answers the visitor's question.

## Evidence

- The existing public page code used `practical AI` and `AI for small business` extensively.
- The exact phrases `start a business`, `build an app`, and `app idea` did not appear in public page copy before this work.
- U.S. Census Business Formation Statistics reported 531,423 seasonally adjusted business applications in June 2026.
- Google Search Essentials recommends using the words people use in prominent page locations while keeping content helpful and people-first.
- Google's generative AI search guidance recommends original, experience-led content instead of commodity pages or pages made for every keyword variation.

## Positioning

NTA should not promise easy money, instant apps, or AI shortcuts.

The NTA promise for aspiring entrepreneurs is:

> Turn an idea, skill, or app concept into a better-understood business by building the customer, offer, trust, and operating foundation before buying more tools.

## Search language by fit

### Primary language

- how to start a small business
- first steps to starting a business
- how to turn an idea into a business
- small-business startup guide
- business foundations for entrepreneurs
- practical business advice for first-time owners
- how to validate a business idea
- how to start a business with AI
- practical AI for entrepreneurs
- how to use AI for a small business

### Supporting language

- how to build an app for my business
- how to turn an app idea into a business
- should I build an app for my business
- build a business app without coding
- business systems for startups
- start a one-person business
- turn experience into a business
- turn a skill into a business

These phrases should support pages that answer the full business question. NTA should not create thin pages for every wording variation.

### Language to use carefully

- make money from an idea
- make money with AI
- side hustle
- passive income
- best AI tools

These phrases can attract shortcut-seeking or tool-shopping intent. They may appear naturally when NTA directly challenges the shortcut and explains that useful value, trust, and follow-through create revenue.

### Language to avoid targeting

- get rich quick
- guaranteed income
- passive income with no work
- AI money hack
- instant business

These promises conflict with NTA's voice and trust position.

## Initial page architecture

1. `/start-a-business` — hub for aspiring entrepreneurs and first-time owners.
2. The Better Business Book — deeper business-foundation education.
3. Practical AI for Small Business — responsible AI use after the business purpose is understood.
4. Growth Guide — guided discovery and next-step clarity.
5. Future experience-led lessons:
   - What to Understand Before You Start a Business
   - How to Test a Business Idea Without Wasting Money
   - Build the Business System Before You Buy the Tools
   - Can AI Help Me Start a Business?
   - How to Turn an App Idea Into a Real Business
   - Why Trust Matters Before the First Sale
   - From Idea to First Customer

## Measurement

After the new public app is live:

1. Submit the updated sitemap in Google Search Console.
2. Track impressions and queries for the startup and entrepreneur cluster.
3. Review which queries lead to engaged visits, book downloads, Growth Guide starts, and conversations.
4. Expand only the themes that attract people NTA can genuinely teach or serve.

Do not treat rankings or traffic alone as success. The useful outcome is discovery by people who value practical education, honest guidance, and responsible implementation.
